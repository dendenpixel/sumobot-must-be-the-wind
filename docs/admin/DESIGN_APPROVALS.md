# Design approvals

What the course coordinator approved on the rules, and when. The design was emailed in early September and the answers came back over the following fortnight.

## 8 Sep 2026

- **Floor suction / downforce fan: approved.** The caveat was that the competition timber plate isn't perfectly smooth, so expect side-leak losses. The skirt & seal were designed for a floor that isn't flat as a result.
- **"Out of scope"** means destructive actions toward the opponent only (no "robot war"), so pushing, wedging & lifting were fine.
- A CO2 flipper would also have been allowed with an extra safety assessment, which didn't matter in the end because CO2 was already dropped on 2 Sep.

## Confirmed by 23 Sep 2026

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the ESC count as an allowed "motor controller"? | **Yes** |
| Can the fan pre-spool before the start? | **Yes** |
| Can the repo go public after the course? | **Yes**, once it's been cleared of anything private |

Nothing was still open on the rules after 23 Sep.
